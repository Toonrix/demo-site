Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good fit?

UK fire & life safety (ITM) firms — sprinkler, fire alarm, detection, suppression and maintenance contractors — with an average account value above ~£10K who've grown mostly on referrals and tenders and want a pipeline they control. One rule: you need room to take on new maintenance accounts right now. If your crews are already at capacity, we're not a fit — we won't book meetings you can't service.

How quickly will we see results?

Most clients see their first booked meetings within 14–21 days of setup. That window covers dedicated domain provisioning, inbox warm-up, and building and validating your first target list.

How are you different from a standard lead generation agency?

Most agencies buy one big generic list, blast it, and forward you every reply that comes back so the report looks busy. We target on live compliance and renewal signals, write to what a Responsible Person actually cares about, qualify hard before anyone reaches your calendar, and take our fee on held meetings only. If you've paid a retainer before and got a spreadsheet of "leads" who never answered the phone — that's the model we're built against.

What is your guarantee?

It's a performance-linked partnership: no ongoing retainer, and you only pay for meetings that are actually held with a qualified decision-maker. If we don't book the agreed number of qualified meetings in a billing cycle, we keep working — at no additional tech or management fee — until we do. Your side of the deal: show up to the meetings, reply to warm leads within a day, and let the campaign run.

Do you work with my competitors?

No. We work with one fire & life safety firm per territory. Once you're onboarded, your patch is locked — we turn away every competing firm in your service radius for as long as we work together. The pipeline we build is yours alone, which is exactly why we cap onboarding at two slots a month.

Is this compliant and safe for our brand?

Yes. We operate within UK GDPR and PECR — B2B outreach to corporate subscribers on a legitimate-interest basis — and because we use isolated, dedicated sending domains, your primary company domain and IT infrastructure stay 100% insulated from all outbound activity.
