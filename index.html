We don't blast a bought list. We find SMBs with 5–100 W2 employees whose policies renew inside 90 days — or who just raised, acquired, or expanded and need coverage they don't have yet. Your producer only ever sits across from someone with a real reason to move.

The Right Decision-Maker

Owners, founders, presidents, CFOs, risk managers — the people who can actually say yes to a BOR change or a new program. Not an office manager who has to "check with someone."

Exclusive, Never Shared

Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good fit?

Independent P&C and commercial-lines agencies with at least one full-time producer and the appetite to write more small-to-mid-market business. We target SMB accounts with 5–100 W2 employees, so if that's in your wheelhouse, you're a fit.

One rule: your producers need the capacity to take the meetings. If they're already maxed, we're not a fit — we won't book appointments your team can't work.

How does the guarantee work?

It's performance-based. You're guaranteed a minimum of 3 qualified meetings per producer each month — or, for agencies with 4 or more producers, a minimum of 15 a month across the team. If we don't hit it, we make it right.

Your side of the deal keeps it fair: your producers show up to the meetings we book, you reply to interested prospects within a business day, and you give us your appetite and suppression list up front. Hit those, and we hit the minimum — simple as that.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A live meeting with a real decision-maker — owner, founder, president, CFO or risk manager — at an SMB with 5–100 W2 employees whose coverage renews inside the next 90 days, or who has a genuine open need (a recent raise, acquisition or expansion). They have to actually attend and engage. Anyone who isn't a fit doesn't count and isn't billed.

How are the leads sourced — and are they really exclusive?

Yes, completely exclusive. We never share or resell an account to another agency, broker or carrier — when your producer walks in, they're not competing with three other quotes we also sold.

We don't just run the same bought list everyone else uses. We identify accounts entering their renewal window and layer in live signals — funding rounds, acquisitions, expansions — so you're reaching businesses with a real, current reason to talk.

What's included, and how much is it?

It's fully done-for-you: dedicated sending domains and inboxes, the account data and lists, copywriting, multi-channel outreach (email, LinkedIn, phone), qualification, and calendar booking. You don't build or touch anything.

Pricing is performance-based and depends on your producer count and setup, so we scope it on a quick call rather than quoting blind. Book a call and we'll walk you through exactly what it'd look like for your agency.

Do you work with my competitors?

No. One agency per market. Once you're on board, your market is locked — we turn away competing agencies in your area for as long as we work together, on top of every individual account being exclusive to you.

Is this compliant and safe for our agency?

Yes. We operate within CAN-SPAM — accurate sender identity, a real physical address, and a working opt-out on every send — and because we run dedicated sending domains, your agency's real domain and deliverability stay completely insulated.
