Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good fit?

Expert B2B service firms with an average client or deal value above ~$10K who've grown mostly on referrals and want a pipeline they control. One rule: you need room to take on new clients right now. If you're already at capacity, we're not a fit — we won't book calls you can't service.

How quickly will we see results?

Most clients see their first booked calls within 14–21 days of setup. That window covers dedicated domain provisioning, inbox warm-up, and building and validating your first target list.

How are you different from a standard lead generation agency?

Most agencies blast one big generic list and forward you every reply that comes back. We target on live buying signals, write to your buyer's actual pain, qualify hard before anyone reaches your calendar, and tie our fee to hitting your quota — which most agencies won't do.

What is your guarantee?

It's a performance-linked partnership. If we don't book the agreed number of qualified calls in a billing cycle, we keep working — completely free — until we do. Your side of the deal: show up to the calls, reply to warm leads within a day, and let the campaign run. We tie our success directly to your pipeline.

Is this compliant and safe for our brand?

Yes. We operate within CAN-SPAM and GDPR, and because we use isolated, dedicated sending domains, your primary corporate domain and IT infrastructure stay 100% insulated from all outbound activity.
