The Difference

Built for Fire & Security Firms, Not Generic "Leads"

Compliance-Led Targeting

We don't blast a big list. We look for Estates, Block and Facilities Managers with a live problem. An overdue service. A contract up for renewal. New buildings on the books. Or a site that falls under the Building Safety Act. So you only ever speak to someone who already has to buy this.

Copy Written to Their Pain

We write about what a Responsible Person actually worries about. An overdue BS 5839 service. A fire risk assessment with actions nobody closed off. A notice from the fire and rescue authority. A contractor who bills for remedial works but never turns up to do them. That's what makes someone stop and reply.

Total Domain Isolation

We set up separate sending domains and warm them up properly. That way your emails land in the main inbox, not spam. And your company domain stays completely clean. No risk to your brand.

Ruthless Qualification

We don't forward every reply that comes in. We check each one against your rules first. Right kind of property. Inside your patch. A real contract or compliance window open. Only then does it reach your diary.

Booked to Your Calendar

Checked, ready decision-makers land straight in your diary. With notes on what they need. You just turn up.

Full Transparency

You get a live dashboard. Campaign health, replies, and meetings booked. You see everything we see. No black box.

Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is a good fit?

UK fire & security firms. Fire alarm and detection, sprinkler, suppression, emergency lighting, plus intruder alarms, CCTV and access control. If you install and service any mix of these, you're a fit. Your average account should be worth around £10k or more. You've grown on referrals and tenders, and you want a pipeline you control.

One rule: you need room to take on new work right now. If your engineers are flat out, we're not a fit. We won't book meetings you can't service.

How quickly will we see results?

Most clients get their first meetings booked within 14 to 21 days. That covers setting up your domains, warming up the inboxes, and building your first list.

How are you different from a standard lead generation agency?

Most agencies buy one big list, blast it, then forward you every reply so the report looks busy.

We do the opposite. We target on live compliance and renewal signals. We write about what a Responsible Person actually cares about. We qualify hard before anyone reaches your diary. And we only get paid for meetings that are held.

If you've paid a retainer before and got a spreadsheet of "leads" who never picked up — that's exactly what we're built against.

What is your guarantee?

No retainer. You only pay for meetings that are actually held with a qualified decision-maker.

If we don't hit the agreed number in a billing cycle, we keep working for free until we do. No extra tech or management fees, ever.

Your side of the deal: turn up to the meetings, reply to warm leads within a day, and let the campaign run.

Do you work with my competitors?

No. One fire & security firm per area. Once you're on board, your patch is locked. We turn away every competing firm in your radius for as long as we work together.

The pipeline we build is yours alone. That's why we only take two slots a month.

Is this compliant and safe for our brand?

Yes. We work within UK GDPR and PECR. That means B2B outreach to business contacts on a legitimate interest basis.

And because we use separate sending domains, your company domain and IT stay completely out of it.
